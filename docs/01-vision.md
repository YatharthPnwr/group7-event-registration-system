## Vision statement

For **college students and tech communities**
who struggle with **scattered sign-up forms and manual confirmations**,
**Event Registration System** is a **web application**
that **lets users register for events in seconds and gives organisers one place to manage attendees**.
Unlike **spreadsheets and generic forms**,
our product **enforces seat limits and sends confirmation emails automatically**.

## Product goal

A working, tested and deployed prototype by **15 Nov 2026**. It covers student registration, admin management of registrations, and confirmation emails.

## Users

| User | What they need |
|---|---|
| Student | Find events, register quickly, get proof of registration, cancel if plans change |
| Admin (organiser) | Create and edit events, see who registered, remove registrations, make sure everyone got a confirmation |

## Scope

**In this release:** accounts and roles, events, registration with seat limits, admin management of registrations, confirmation email, CI, automated tests, deployment.

**Not in this release:** payments, QR tickets, attendance check-in, mobile app.
